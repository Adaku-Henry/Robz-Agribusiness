BUSINESS PLAN
 
ROBZ-AGRIBUSINESS SMC LIMITED  
 
1. Executive Summary 
Robz Agribusiness SMC ltd is a youth led single member business enterprise, that 
started in 2024, June, 10th. And located at Ocodri Trading Centre, Bileafe sub-cunty 
Terego District. The business offers the following farm inputs, consultation, extension 
services, deliveries supply of farmer groups and market linkages.   
The agribusiness input shop is serving the local individual farmer, schools, farmer 
groups, NGOs, partners and contract service farming. Within 12–18 months, Robz 
Agribusiness is projected to break even and grow into a full agribusiness company and 
registered under URSB. 
 
Vision. 
  To uplift small stalk holder farmers to the world.   
 
Mission.   
  To become the leading agribusiness company in Ajiraku parish, Bileafe sub-county, 
Terego and by time to the whole parts of west Nile and Uganda.  
 
2. Business Objectives 
• To uplift small stakeholder farmers to the world. 
• To solve the community farmers challenges and needs  
• To become the leading agribusiness company in Terego District. 
• To promote sustainable farming through climate smart agriculture (Trainings) 
 
3. Products & Services 
• Farm inputs (chemicals, farm tools, pesticides, herbicides, fungicides and 
seeds)

• Extension services (offering trainings for farmers and farmer groups) 
• Consultation (making farmers to understand commercial/farming as business)

o farmer groups  
o  NGOs and partners

o Affordable pricing with wide service variety. 
o Professional branding under Robz agribusiness. 
 
5. Marketing Strategy 
• Attractive shop branding & signboard banners/pull up banner. 
• Word-of-mouth campaigns and satisfied customer referrals. 
• Follow up and feedback of customers as action part. 
• Partnerships with schools, farmer groups, farmer organizations, NGOs and local 
government offices for bulk production hence returns to Purch. 
• Offering trainings for farmers
